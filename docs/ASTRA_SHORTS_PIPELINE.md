## Stage order and acceptance

1. **Designer:** one compact story card per Short, explicit object inventory,
   causal beats, original comedy/sound and likely misreads. Apply all relevant
   requirements in the independent concept-safety report.
2. **Animator/scripts:** solve vertical stage intents and prototype the critical
   actions. First: gentle ball contact after paw withdrawal, then Pip's complete
   pickup/deposit/retrieval. Use shallow lidless homes, substantial props and
   clear routes; stop ball play while tidying.
3. **Independent plan safety and blind storyboard review:** safety receives the
   measured plan; a fresh blind reviewer receives only a neutral covered sheet.
   Preserve original retellings and compare problem/cause/lesson separately.
4. **Animator/render scripts:** build that Short directly with explicit shot
   inputs and continuous original audio. Reuse checked performances/assets where
   suitable; each vertical camera remains independently checked.
5. **Technical checks:** actual encoded duration, video/audio, safe zones,
   framing, whole-mask clearance, contact/custody continuity and relevant
   regression checks on that exact revision. Every Short gets its own evidence.
6. **Fresh blind video review:** overall and story clarity at least 8/10 within
   two rounds; preserve failures. Safety and technical failures are never waived.
7. **Packaging and final independent safety:** only after that Short's final
   media exists; bind reports to its exact media and text.
8. **Owner:** fresh approval before any publication. Later growth review uses
   authorized real Shorts data at comparable ages, never fabricated observations.

Complete and verify the first Short before scaling the same solution to the
second. Parallel work is limited to independent reviews with real inputs.
No blind reviewer receives this document or the story card.
