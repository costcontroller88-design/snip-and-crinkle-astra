## Phase 2: Build the library (mid-Nov 2026 – 4 Jan 2027)
- About 1 mini-episode and 3 Shorts a week, following the topic pipeline below.
- Winter special by about 20 Dec. First 20–30 min compilation in late December (holiday TV viewing), made from about
  10 new-style mini-episodes, with its own intro and outro.
- Market scan early each month (next: early Nov).
- Day-90 check, 4 Jan 2027: under 100 subscribers OR under 50 long-form hours → keep the characters, redesign the
  format, and present 3 options with ratings.
- Planning targets for 4 Jan: about 10 mini-episodes, 1 compilation, about 40 Shorts in total, 300–1,000
  subscribers, 100–400 long-form watch hours.

## Phase 3: Scale to the Partner Program (Jan – Dec 2027)
- 3 Shorts and 1–2 long-form videos a week. Rotate compilation lengths (about 8, 20–30 and 45–60 min); add themed and
  seasonal marathons.
- Weekly watch-hour forecast against 8,000 (rolling 12 months). Monthly scorecard: hours, subscribers, top 5 and
  bottom 5 videos, actions.
- The math: 8,000 hours = 480,000 minutes, which is about 60,000 views at an 8-minute average (compilations) or
  240,000 views at 2 minutes (mini-episodes alone). Compilations carry the hours.

## Phase 4: Brand and licensing (from about 100 episodes, or earlier if the data is strong)

  - series bible;

  - list of 5–11 minute landscape episodes, with lessons and ages;
  - the "no dubbing needed" pitch;
  - audience numbers.

  Targets: Kidoodle.TV, Amazon Kids+, Roku Channel, Samsung TV Plus, regional OTT services. Outreach emails are
  drafted for the owner's "yes".
- Trademark registration in India through a professional.
- A printables page for parents and teachers (adults only, no data from children); parent-facing social clips only.
