# Benchmark: Halloween for little kids, for Episode 11 (6 Oct 2026)

Focused scan for Episode 11 (a gentle, wordless Halloween special for ages 2-6, to be uploaded by 28 Oct 2026).
Read-only. Companion to `docs/benchmark-oct-2026.md` (the general scan, same day).

**How this was read.** YouTube pages in the built-in browser, logged out, region IN, on 2026-10-06: channel Videos and
Shorts tabs ("Latest" and, where noted, "Popular"), plus YouTube searches sorted by view count with the "this month" /
"this year" filters. Exact dates, lengths and view counts come from each video's own page data (not from the rounded
"3 weeks ago" labels). Preview strips (1 frame a second for Shorts, 1 frame per 5-10 s for long videos) and
thumbnails were looked at to see what happens and how dark or spooky it is. Calendar dates and the guidance quotes come
from web pages listed under Sources. Where I could not confirm something it says "not verified".

Tabs read: Popular = Oddbods videos, Booba videos. Latest = Oddbods videos and Shorts, Larva TUBA, Booba, BabyBus,
MEOWJI Shorts, KIDZOKI Shorts. Halloween-specific = a YouTube search per channel (sorted by views), because most channel
pages show no Halloween video in their newest 30 uploads.

---

## 0. The answer in ten lines

1. **Two kinds of "new" reach.** New Halloween uploads from song and play brands get millions in 10-25 days (Hogi 7.62M
   in 25 days, Bebefinn 3.07M in 10, CoComelon 2.04M in 17). New Halloween story cartoons get tens of thousands
   (Booba 58k-91k, Oddbods 22k-33k). We are a wordless story cartoon: plan for the second band.
2. **The biggest Halloween Shorts are not carving; they are "friendly glow" crafts.** KIDZOKI's glowing green
   night-light 329M (30 Oct 2025, 19 s) and MEOWJI's glowing balloon lamp 268.6M (19 Oct 2025, 30 s). Animated
   pumpkin-carving Shorts from famous kids' brands got 7k-1.2M.
3. **Pumpkin (plain) to glowing face is a common idea, not a proven top one.** The best case I found is a 15 s
   live-action clay pumpkin that ends glowing, 16.6M (Tuta Cute Art, 27 Oct 2025). Kids' brands' character versions
   (Pinkfong, CoComelon, Oddbods, Blippi, Peppa) got 7k-1.2M. Detail in section 2.
4. **Gentle is the norm for ages 2-6.** Toddler brands use bright pictures, round big-eyed monsters, white dot-eyed
   ghosts, costumes, pumpkins, candy mysteries; they write "Spooky", not "Scary". Dark palettes, fangs and curses
   appear only on the older-kid shows (Oddbods "Party Monsters", Larva).
5. **Timing.** BabyBus (13 Sep), Hogi (11 Sep), CoComelon (19 Sep) and Oddbods Shorts (19 Aug) started Halloween weeks ago.
   Last year's two hit Shorts went up 11-16 days before Halloween (15-20 Oct 2025), and one hit went up on 30 Oct.
   28 Oct is inside the season but late; Halloween 2026 is Saturday 31 Oct, only 25 days from today (under our 4-week rule).
6. **"Autumn" beat "Halloween" on Booba this year**: "Hello, Autumn!" 253.9k vs "Halloween Magic" 69.5k.
7. **Songs dominate search.** On the first page for "halloween cartoon for kids" about 13 of 21 results are songs or
   nursery rhymes; wordless cartoons are almost absent (Booba only).
8. **Glowing jack-o'-lanterns sell the click**: they are on the thumbnails of 3 of the top 5 all-time Halloween kids'
   videos (Infobells, Super Simple Songs, Kiddiestv) and on Booba's newest Halloween thumbnails.
9. **Nothing else in the data says "dark" helps.** The two 100M+ glow Shorts are shot in a bedroom at night, but the
   light is the hero, and the "monster" is a fluffy big-eyed toy.
10. **What is not proven:** that any of this works for a brand-new channel with no audience. Even the giants' numbers
    swing 80x for the same craft on the same day (section 1D).

## 3. Gentle versus spooky

**What I saw the toddler-aimed channels do (6 Oct 2026; titles, thumbnails, descriptions and preview strips, not whole
videos):**
- Bright, saturated pictures with big round faces (CoComelon, Bebefinn, Hogi, BabyBus, Peppa, Mickey). Ghosts are
  white blobs with dot eyes; monsters are round, green and big-eyed; "zombie" means a cute green blob.
- Daylight or warm indoor light. The darkest toddler thumbnail is BabyBus's moonlit pumpkin patch, and it is full of
  smiling jack-o'-lanterns.
- Words: "Spooky", "Costume Party", "Pumpkin Time", "Who took the candy?", "Good Morning", "Not-So-Scary". Booba's
  2026 descriptions say "playful", "cozy", "autumn". The older, bigger songs do say "Scary" (Teehee Town "Spooky Scary
  Skeletons" 495.7M), so "scary" can still sell, but the toddler brands' 2026 uploads I read lean on "spooky".
- Story shape: the monsters are silly or lost, nobody is hurt, and the ending is kind (Monster Police: monsters are
  taught to fix mistakes). Candy mysteries, costume parties and pumpkin patches are the safe core.
- Not seen on the toddler brands' new thumbnails: blood, fangs, dark rooms, realistic skulls, clowns, hands reaching,
  jump-scare faces. Seen only on older-kid shows: fangs and glowing yellow eyes with dark purple and navy (Oddbods
  "Party Monsters" thumbnail and palette), compilations called "SCARY NIGHT" and "VAMPIRE" (Larva, 2017).

**What the experts say** (AAP HealthyChildren.org and Child Mind Institute, read 6 Oct 2026): babies, toddlers and
preschoolers often can't tell fantasy from reality, so scary experiences can spoil Halloween; Halloween should be
presented as pretend ("the scary stuff is all pretend"); what frightens small children is spooky decorations
(skeletons, ghosts, graveyards), masks and motion-activated props; masks should not block sight or breathing.

**What YouTube says** (Child safety policy, "misleading family content", read 6 Oct 2026, as summarised by the fetch
tool; check the wording before quoting it): content aimed at young children must not have "adult horror characters" or
other themes meant to shock young viewers. A gentle story is in line with this; a dark one is a policy risk for a
Made for Kids channel.

**Not verified:** any channel's internal rule; what happens inside the long episodes (I looked at descriptions and
preview strips only).

## 6. What it means for Episode 11

**Recommendation (firm).** Make Episode 11 a "friendly glow" story, not a pumpkin-carving lesson.

- **Payoff.** A plain pumpkin gets a face by paper and paint (nothing sharp is shown in a child's paw), and then it
  glows (a battery light placed by the grown-up's paper hand). This is the one visual that all the evidence supports:
  glow is the payoff of the 329M and 268.6M Shorts (and of the 16.6M clay pumpkin), and a lit jack-o'-lantern is on the
  thumbnails of 3 of the top 5 Halloween kids' videos.
- **Heart.** "Fear becomes fun": a small, unsure friend (Pip) is wary of a shadow, and Snip and Crinkle make a friendly
  light. Kindness toward someone who is afraid, with "it's all pretend" shown as an action. This fits our edge (heart)
  and the adult guidance, and it echoes Monster Police (230.7M), the only large Halloween video with a kindness lesson.
  Engine: E (looking after Pip) or C fits; it must differ from the pilot's engine. The story-designer decides.
- **Gentle rules.** Bright, warm, daylit or lamplit scenes, no dark room, no masks that hide faces, no jump scare, no
  fangs or glowing eyes, no real flame or knife. Ghosts and monsters, if any, are round, soft and friendly. Use the word
  "spooky" at most.
- **Shorts.** Transformation Short of 20-30 s (the hits run 19-30 s): plain pumpkin to glowing friend, with the glow in
  the first 2 s as a teaser or the plain pumpkin in the first frame (MEOWJI opened with the glowing result; KIDZOKI
  opened with the problem; both worked). A second, optional Short: a costume reveal (a paw jumps, a colour, a costume),
  which got 315.5k in 4 days for KIDZOKI.
- **Length.** The 2-4 minute landscape episode sits where the winning long Halloween videos sit (Booba "Halloween
  Adventure" 6:56 gets 31.3M; the big songs run 2-8 minutes).
- **Title style.** Lead with the result, say "Halloween" and "Pumpkin" in plain words, one emoji pair, no "Scary", no
  other brands. Examples: "Pumpkin Glow-Up! 🎃✨ | Snip & Crinkle | Kids Cartoon" (episode); "Plain Pumpkin Becomes a
  Glowing Friend! 🎃✨ #shorts" (Short). Mention "autumn" in the description (Booba's "Hello, Autumn!" beat its
  Halloween videos by about 3.5x).
- **Timing.** Ship the Shorts as early as the schedule allows, then the episode; 28 Oct is the last good day, not
  the best one. Last year's hit Shorts went up 11-16 days before Halloween.
- **Expectation.** The giants' new story-cartoon Halloween uploads got 16k-91k in 2-20 days. Judge Episode 11 against
  that band, not against the toddler-song numbers (0.5M-7.6M) and not against the 268M/329M Shorts. With under 300
  views, say "too early".
