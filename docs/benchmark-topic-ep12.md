## Three pairs, ranked

**1. Lesson: put things back where they live (tidy up together). Demand: colour/shape sorting into baskets.**
- Wrong, then right: Snip pulls out every toy and Pip's yellow ball goes missing; Snip digs through the heap and makes it worse
  (a toy drum rolls on top of the ball). Crinkle sets out three baskets (circle, square, star picture on each); Snip puts the toys in, counting 1, 2, 3,
  and the ball appears at once; the game starts. Cause is visible, nobody is blamed, nobody trips.
- Why: the visible messy-to-tidy change is the Shorts pattern that wins (MEOWJI, KIDZOKI colour fills); tidy-up has the freshest new-upload proof (Ozzy Fox 7.1M at 2:31).
  It is wordless-friendly (sorting by picture and colour). Dated 13 Nov World Kindness Day: "helping" fits.
- Shorts: (a) 15 s "Messy floor to tidy: toys fly into baskets" (one transformation); (b) 15 s "Where is the ball?" (the hunt, the tidy, the find).
- Risks: small-channel tidy-up videos get almost nothing (24 views), so the Short must be built on the satisfying sort, not the lesson.

**2. Lesson: brush teeth before bed. Demand: sparkle (clean-up transformation).**
- Wrong, then right: Snip skips it with a sticky jam smudge on a tooth; the tooth looks dull in the mirror; Crinkle's little brush circles it and the tooth sparkles.
- Why: highest absolute views of all candidates (many 13-21M this quarter) and strong parent search. Weakness: song-dominated, and hygiene was Episode 2's category; the sparkle Short is strong, but the mini-episode would face Ms Rachel and Sesame.
- Risk: no decay or pain imagery (not scary); keep the smudge as stickiness only.

**3. Lesson: try one bite of a new food. Demand: rainbow vegetables.**
- Wrong, then right: Crinkle pushes the green plate away, arms crossed; Pip nibbles a pea; Crinkle tries one bite and his eyes sparkle.
- Why: the largest parent search of the three ("toddler won't eat"). Weakness: the weakest video proof (cartoon picky-eater videos 550-974 views; the one hit is a live-action-style story), and the "wrong" moment risks laughing at the unsure one, which the rules ban. Keep it as a later Short, not the episode.

Runner-up not listed: bedtime/"toddler won't sleep" (PBS Lyla 2.6M; lullaby 2.3M) is demand-rich but too close to Episode 11's dusk and dark.

## Recommendation (firm)
Make Episode 12 "Tidy up together", paired with colour/shape sorting. Open on the problem (the lost ball, in the first 5 s), one gag every 15-20 s, ending on a
satisfying all-tidy shot. Release Short (a) first, as a standalone transformation. Check on day 7 and 28: if the tidy Short is swiped away above 60 percent,
swap the next Short for the teeth-sparkle idea (pair 2).

## Potty: "notice the feeling, go in time" (added 8 Oct)
**Advice frame.** NHS (https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/bedwetting/, read 8 Oct): bedwetting is common; many children under 5 wet the bed; "do not punish your child - it is not their fault"; go to the toilet regularly, including just before bedtime; do not regularly wake or carry a child at night. So the lesson is: in the day, notice the wiggle and go in time; at bedtime, "potty before bed" is a calm routine, never a test. I did not read AAP; the AAP point (night dryness comes with maturity) is not checked.

**Demand (views on 8 Oct, search sorted by views, this year).** Songs lead: Wolfoo "Push The Button" 26M (5mo), NiNiPhant Hindi 22M (5mo), Bebefinn "Poo Poo Song" 17M and 14M (4-5mo), Tidi Kids 13M (9mo), Cam & Sam "Go to the Potty" 5.4M (3mo, 19:37). The one story cartoon, Doo Bee Doo Land "Where Is My Potty?" 12M (8mo). Within 90 days only Cam & Sam is large; a small channel's potty song got 2 views. Autocomplete (order, not volume): "potty training songs toddlers", "potty song ms rachel", "potty training videos", "potty before bed song"; "bedwetting stories".

**Wordless and gentle?** Yes, if: clothes stay on; the feeling is shown as a wiggle, crossed paws and a "?" bubble, never body detail; the potty is a plain chair-like pot; an accident is one small puddle that Crinkle meets with a cloth and a hug, nobody laughs, nobody scolds; the grown-up's paper hand helps. No "poo" jokes (all top songs use them; we would not). Made for Kids: allowed topic; keep it non-graphic. Risk: it is a sensitive, medical-adjacent topic and the winners rely on poo humour we cannot use.

**Shorts fit:** weak. No visible transformation; one option is a 15 s "Wiggle, wiggle, go!" run-and-sticker beat.

**Rank:** 2. Highest parent demand, but song-dominated, thinner fresh proof and higher sensitivity than tidy-up. Safety-checker must pass the plan first.
