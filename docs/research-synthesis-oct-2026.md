# Research synthesis: topics Nov 2026 - Jan 2027 (Lead, 8 Oct 2026)

Owner asked for "everything, no cap". Four parallel growth-analyst runs, ~540k tokens, 5-hour limit 31 → 53%,
weekly 56 → 59%. Sources: `docs/research-lessons-oct-2026.md` (17 lessons by views), `docs/research-wordless-craft-oct-2026.md`
(10 craft rules), `docs/research-demand-seasons-oct-2026.md` (searches + calendar), `docs/research-policy-safety-oct-2026.md`
(policy + safety per lesson), and the first pass `docs/benchmark-topic-ep12.md`.

## What the four agree on
- **Clarity is a policy issue, not taste:** "hard to follow" is a named low-quality signal for kids' content; the
  "templated" rule is still our biggest policy risk (every episode must differ in substance).
- **Big view counts belong to songs from giant channels**; small channels' lesson videos get 30-3,000 views. No
  wordless lesson video surfaced at all: an open lane or no demand, unknown. Plan for the story-cartoon band.
- **Habit searches are flat through the year (±15%)**: a season adds a date to aim at, not extra demand.
- **The craft fixes Episode 11 lacked:** the problem in frame 0; cause before effect with one object; every beat
  changes the situation (no 6 s without change); a face close-up after every gag; the lesson as an object that
  changes hands; a Short is one beat of 20-30 s.

## Decision
Episode 12 stays **"Tidy up together"**, now confirmed by three of four scans: an evergreen episode (long-form,
watch hours) and its transformation Short dressed for Diwali ("tidy floor, then rangoli", LED lamps, no fire), Tue 3 Nov
6 PM IST. Then Episode 13 potty in time, Episode 14 bedtime, Episode 15 dressed warm (special Sat 12 Dec), Episode 16
teeth + New Year. Engines rotate (the designer picks; Crinkle or both err in at least one of 12-14).
