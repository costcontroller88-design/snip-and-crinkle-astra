# Parent demand and seasons, Nov 2026 - Jan 2027 (growth-analyst, 8 Oct 2026)

Decision first: pick lessons for demand, then wrap them in a season; habit searches barely move through the year (swing about +/-15%), so a season adds a date, not demand. Best three pairs are in the last section.

## 2. What parents search (autocomplete, 8 Oct 2026)

- Google US: "toddler won't eat / stay in bed / sleep / poop"; "toddler hates brushing teeth / baths / hair washing"; "my toddler refuses to use the potty"; "2 year old won't stop hitting". India adds "won't sit on potty", "baby bedtime stories" (also Hindi, Telugu), "kids habits list".
- YouTube suggestions (US and India): "potty training song/videos", "brush teeth song", "clean up song", "wash hands song", "bedtime stories", "sharing is caring song", and in India "good habits cartoon / rhymes / song". Searchers expect a song; a wordless film must win on picture and title ("... cartoon for kids").

## 5. Pairs (lesson first, then demand/season)

1. **Tidy up + Diwali.** Lesson: tidy the floor together (Episode 12). Demand: "clean up song" is the top US habit term and tidy-up is the least-served; cleaning the home before Diwali is a real custom, so the Short "tidy floor, then rangoli" with LED lamps ties both. Short, Tue 3 Nov, 6 PM IST (ramp from Sun 1 Nov, peak week 8 Nov). Backup Thu 5 Nov.
2. **Getting dressed warm (hat, scarf, mittens) + winter/Christmas season.** Lesson: dress for the cold, Crinkle's scarf shared with Pip; demand: Cocomelon's get-dressed hits (12.8M) have no wordless version; season peak is the week of 20 Dec. Winter Shorts Tue 1 - Fri 18 Dec; mini-episode/special Sat 12 Dec, then the compilation 21-26 Dec. A 20 Dec upload would arrive on the peak with no time to be recommended; earlier is better for a channel with no subscribers.
3. **Brushing teeth + New Year habits.** Lesson: brush morning and night; demand: brushing is the top YouTube habit term in the UK, India, Philippines and Brazil (53-76), and the 132M KIDZOKI Short shows a Short can carry it; season: a one-week spike, a 3-2-1 countdown (numerals allowed). Short Sat 26 - Tue 29 Dec (spike week starts Sun 27 Dec).

Also: **hand washing after puddle play + rainy day** (Short, any week from 16 Nov; strongest in Indonesia/Brazil). **Potty in time** (Episode 13) has the biggest parent demand but is song-crowded and has no season; ship it as evergreen. **Bedtime** is the best long-form (watch-hours) candidate: a calm 20-30 min collection for the late-December TV period (estimate; no wordless bedtime competitor appeared in search).

Short-only evidence: Diwali rangoli, New Year countdown, brushing teeth, hand washing (KIDZOKI-style one change). Episode-capable: tidy up, winter dressing, bedtime.

## 6. Method and gaps

Google Trends (Web and YouTube search, 5 years; US, UK, India, Philippines, Indonesia, Brazil; local terms for ID and BR), Google/YouTube suggest, YouTube search pages with watch-page publish dates, 8 Oct 2026. Values are relative; terms under 1 are noise. Not checked: Studio data, Hari Guru upload patterns, 12-month charts outside the US.
