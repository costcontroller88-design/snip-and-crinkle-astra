# Lesson demand on YouTube, ages 2-6 (8 Oct 2026)

## Decision
Rank by how much YouTube demand a visible, wordless, safe change can ride on. **Top 5: 1 Bedtime routine, 2 Potty in time, 3 Teeth, 4 Bath / hair wash, 5 Brave at the doctor.** Pair each with a demand dress below. Next: losing a game, big sibling and baby (Pip), getting dressed ("I can do it myself"). Two warnings: no *wordless* lesson video surfaced in any search (every hit is a song or a narrated story), so "wordless" is either an open niche or a niche without demand; this scan cannot tell. And the huge Shorts counts are songs by large channels, not proof that a small channel gets them.

## What the numbers say
1. **Routines and body care own Shorts.** The three biggest lesson Shorts of 2026 are all routines: bedtime (410M, 372M), teeth (559M, 543M, 347M) and potty (191M, 99M, 74M). All are 15-60 s, all are songs or one-action clips.
2. **Story-style lessons that work are rare and short.** Toys and Colors' doctor Short (4.98M, 51 s) and Zippie Kids' losing Short (6.06M, 36 s) are single-beat stories, not full plots.
3. **Small channels' lesson videos mostly get 30 to 3,000 views** (losing, friends, helping, new baby Shorts). Only a few get 40K-340K in weeks (Mini Manners doctor 40,528; Agile Mind cupcake 336,394 in 8 days). The topic helps, the format must do the rest.
4. **Long episodes: lesson plus a mess.** "Who Did This?!" 30M (4mo) shows a lesson wrapped in a mystery is allowed to be long. Peppa-type siblings and bedtime titles win on the character IP, so discount them for us.
5. **Songs dominate bath, dressed, potty, teeth; stories dominate losing, siblings, doctor, truth.** A wordless sequence is a third lane; no result in any search exists there.

## Topic dress (lesson first, demand second)
- Bedtime routine + moon/lights-out glow (pairs with winter dusk; Halloween already used for Ep 11).
- Potty in time + "tiny puddle fixed by a hug": Ep 13, needs the NHS tone; keep clothes on.
- Teeth + "sparkle" clean-up (satisfying transformation, 15 s).
- Bath + bubbles: a hair-wash Short with a bubble hat.
- Doctor + sticker: stethoscope and sticker only.

## Which format
Shorts: teeth, bath, getting dressed, bedtime cleanup. Episodes: potty, doctor, big sibling, losing a game. Both: bedtime.

## Cautions
- **Safety:** doctor (no needles or scary tools), bath (shallow, grown-up hand only), potty (no body detail), truth (no scare titles). All five need a safety-check on the plan.
- The "this year" filter mixes up to 12-month-old videos; (90d) marks the recent ones. Counts include replays. Search results show what YouTube chose to surface for my phrasing, not all videos on a lesson.

## Method
Built-in browser, logged out, region as served. YouTube search, filter "this year", sort by view count, type "video" and, for Shorts, the Shorts type filter; top 7-12 rows per lesson. Exact views, upload date and length read by fetching each video's watch page (script read, 8 Oct). ~35 videos checked on the page. Reused: `docs/benchmark-oct-2026.md` and `docs/benchmark-topic-ep12.md` (tidy up, teeth, food, MEOWJI, KIDZOKI, Oddbods). No ROADMAP changes.
