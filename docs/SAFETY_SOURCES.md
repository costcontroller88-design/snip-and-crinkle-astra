## Halloween, pumpkins, light (read 6 Oct 2026 for Episode 11)
- AAP HealthyChildren.org, "Halloween Safety Tips" (updated 29 Sep 2026): small children decorate, not carve; they
  draw faces with markers and an adult does all the cutting; use a battery flameless candle, flashlight or glow stick,
  not a candle. https://www.healthychildren.org/English/safety-prevention/all-around/Pages/Halloween-Safety-Tips.aspx
- AAP HealthyChildren.org, "Easing Infants and Toddlers into Halloween Fun": toddlers need to know scary things are
  pretend; watch for fears. https://www.healthychildren.org/English/safety-prevention/on-the-go/Pages/Easing-Infants-Toddlers-into-Halloween-Fun.aspx
- CPSC Halloween tips (2023): leave carving to adults; children trace or scoop; battery lights, not open flame.
  https://www.cpsc.gov/Newsroom/News-Releases/2023/CPSC-Offers-Safety-Tips-to-Keep-Hazards-from-Haunting-Your-Halloween
- Bradford District Care NHS Foundation Trust (20 Oct 2023): keep children away from knives; painting is a no-carve
  option; LED flameless candles are much safer. https://www.bdct.nhs.uk/about/news/detail/have-fun-stay-safe-at-halloween/

## Button batteries
- AAP HealthyChildren.org, button and coin batteries (lists flameless candles as a source): serious swallowing
  hazard. https://www.healthychildren.org/English/safety-prevention/at-home/Pages/what-parents-need-to-know-about-button-and-lithium-coin-batteries.aspx
- RoSPA, button batteries: flameless candles often lack lockable compartments. https://www.rospa.com/home-safety/product-safety/button-batteries
- Rule we use: only the grown-up's hand touches battery devices; never show one open.

## Fears (dark, noises)
- AAP HealthyChildren.org, "Fears & Phobias in Children": take fears seriously; don't belittle or pressure a child
  to be brave; comfort, light, play. https://www.healthychildren.org/English/health-issues/conditions/emotional-problems/Pages/Understanding-Childhood-Fears-and-Anxieties.aspx
- Zero to Three, fear of the dark at 2: normal; imagination outpaces pretend-vs-real.
  https://www.zerotothree.org/resource/my-2-year-old-son-is-suddenly-afraid-of-the-dark/
- Better Health Channel (Victoria): never ridicule a child's fear. https://www.betterhealth.vic.gov.au/health/conditionsandtreatments/fear-and-anxiety-children
- Rules we use: nobody laughs at the one who is scared; the comfort is light, company and a part to play.

## Lesson topics (read 8 Oct 2026; details in docs/research-policy-safety-oct-2026.md)
- AAP, handwashing (upd. 7 Feb 2022): 20 s soap, dry; keep sanitiser away from children. https://www.healthychildren.org/English/health-issues/conditions/prevention/Pages/Hand-Washing-A-Powerful-Antidote-to-Illness.aspx
- AAP, toothbrushing (upd. 14 May 2025): rice-grain smear under 3, pea from 3; spit, don't rinse; help until about 8. https://www.healthychildren.org/English/healthy-living/oral-health/Pages/Toothbrushing-Tips-for-Young-Children.aspx
- AAP, toilet training age (upd. 24 May 2022): ready 18-24 months; positive, no punishment, shaming or "dirty/naughty". https://www.healthychildren.org/English/ages-stages/toddler/toilet-training/Pages/the-right-age-to-toilet-train.aspx
- Zero to Three, potty training: child-led; no pressure or anger at accidents. https://www.zerotothree.org/resource/potty-training-learning-to-the-use-the-toilet/
- NHS, bedwetting (reviewed 11 Apr 2023; review was due Apr 2026): never punish; common under 5. https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/bedwetting/
- AAP, picky eaters (upd. 26 Apr 2018): 10+ tries, no pressure/bribes, cut food small. https://www.healthychildren.org/English/ages-stages/toddler/nutrition/Pages/Picky-Eaters.aspx
- AAP, bedtime trouble (upd. 25 Aug 2022): quiet routine, same time nightly. https://www.healthychildren.org/English/healthy-living/sleep/Pages/bedtime-trouble.aspx
- Zero to Three, sharing: real sharing at 3.5-4; narrate, timer, long turns, never force. https://www.zerotothree.org/resource/helping-young-children-with-sharing/
- Zero to Three, tantrums 101: stay calm, name feeling, hold the limit; no impulse control until about 3.5-4. https://www.zerotothree.org/resource/toddler-tantrums-101-why-they-happen-and-what-you-can-do/
- NHS children's therapy, getting dressed: let them try first, start with undressing, no time pressure. https://cambspborochildrenshealth.nhs.uk/child-development-and-growing-up/getting-dressed/
