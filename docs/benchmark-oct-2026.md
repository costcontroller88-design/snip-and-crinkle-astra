### Five patterns
1. **The money unit is the long compilation** (15-45 min, up to 2 h) built from many short episodes (1-5 min).
2. **Shorts winners are one simple, satisfying idea**: a visible transformation (dirty to clean, empty to colourful), a test ("which is best?"), or a routine. Not a compressed story.
3. **Topics with demand**: hygiene and routines, colours, food, body parts, babies, rescue/vehicles, and seasonal specials (Halloween, Christmas are Oddbods' top episodes).
4. **Comedy density**: wordless leaders put a gag every 10-20 seconds; characters have strong contrasting personalities; babies and sidekicks add appeal.
5. **Today's reality**: even these giants' NEW uploads get roughly 5k-450k; their totals come from the back catalogue built over years.

## 3. Improvements, in priority order
1. **Native Shorts built on the winning pattern**: one visible, satisfying transformation per Short, tied to a
   habit: "Muddy Paws to Clean Paws!", "Germs Go Away! Brushing Teeth", "Messy Room to Tidy!", "Blank Page to
   Rainbow!". From the pilot: a 15-20 s "Oops! The Splash Becomes a Whale!" (blank, splash, whale, rainbow).
2. **Hook in the first 5-10 s of every episode**: start at the problem (Crinkle already dancing in with the jar),
   not with a calm setup. Pilot act 1 (24 s) should be ~10 s in future episodes.
3. **Comedy every 15-20 s** (rule for every episode plan: at least 5 gags in 2 minutes), built from personality.
4. **Topic pipeline by demand**: hygiene and routines first (hand washing, bath, brushing, bedtime, tidying),
   then colours/painting (our art corner: colour mixing), food (trying new vegetables), body parts, and
   seasonal specials (gentle, not scary: pumpkin painting for Halloween; winter/Christmas).
5. **Pip the duckling as the little one** that Snip and Crinkle look after (babies are among the top topics).
6. **Character bible**: contrasting traits, a running joke each (Crinkle's dance that causes trouble; Snip's ears).
7. **Packaging**: title formula "<Character> <dramatic moment>! <emoji> | Snip & Crinkle | Kids Cartoon" with
   the lesson in the description; wordless thumbnails: big faces, the key object, high contrast, no text.
8. **Library before compilations**: about 10 mini-episodes, then a 20-30 min compilation, then rotate lengths
   like Oddbods.
9. **Expectations**: plan for months of consistent uploads; judge by retention and growth, not single-video views.
