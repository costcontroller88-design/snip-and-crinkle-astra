**Lesson:** Friends (with a grown-up's help) work together to bring back a sad friend's lost "heart light", and the little one gets her turn. This is only partly clear, because the reason Chick is sad is easy to miss.

**Funny moments:** leaf lands on Chick's head (12-16), Bear's tongue-out drawing (54-58), the face glowing on Bear's tummy (124-131), Bear backing away to reveal the giant face (131-133), Chick's peek-a-boo with the wall face (141-150). That is about one every 30 s.

**First 10 s:** a cute chick with a glowing heart on her tummy and a nice sunset colour shift. It is gentle and fairly static, with no question or problem until the hearts fade.

The ending is strong. Light through holes makes shapes, the gate hearts at the start set this up, Chick's heart nose comes back on the wall as a big glowing heart, and the peek-a-boo is a real payoff. The opening "why" undercuts it.

## Blocking
1. **0-12 s: why Chick is sad is likely to be misread.** The heart lights are pale yellow on a cream and peach background (low contrast) and fade slowly along with the whole sunset. Bear's paw on the pumpkin dominates the foreground, and at 16-17 s Bunny points at Bear, who looks guilty and surprised. A child will probably read it as "Bear won't share the pumpkin" or "Bunny tells Bear off", not "Chick lost her heart light". That also hides the heart-nose callback that is the story's point. Fix: higher-contrast hearts, a close-up of Chick watching the heart slide off her tummy and vanish as the sun dips (about 1.5 s or longer), and Bunny pointing at the empty gate or sky instead of at Bear.

## Polish
- 16-31 s: this conversation runs about 15 s with no clear action. Shorten it, or make Bunny's pointing clearly about the missing hearts.
- 32-40 s and 80-84 s: the drawing on the notepad is small. A one-beat close-up would help, and the glow rays on it could be brighter.
- 44-66 s: the drawing section is long and gentle. One small gag would keep the pace up (marker squeak, smudge, Chick sneaking a dot).
- 84-86 s: the human hand appears suddenly in a puppet world. Holding the thumbs-up a beat longer, or showing it at the start, would make "a grown-up helps with the cutting" read better. Keeping the carving off-screen and using an LED light is good and safe.
- 61-63 s: Bear's marker lands under the pumpkin. It looks like a mistake; remove it or use it.
- 131 s: we never see Bear turn the pumpkin to face the wall. The reveal still works, but a quick turn would help the cause-and-effect.
- 141-146 s: the tiny glowing face on Chick's wing is small. The close-up helps; holding it a bit longer would too.
