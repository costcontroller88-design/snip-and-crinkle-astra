## Remaining weaknesses
- The grown-up is shown only as a long arm coming in from the right edge; strictly, a driver would sit to the left of the steering wheel. Kids won't notice, an adult might.
- The pointing finger sits between the two heads and points down at Snip's buckle; it reads as "buckle up", but not as sharply as a finger right on the belt.
- The beach at the end is a hint (palm top above the roof, umbrella in the window), not a full reveal.
- Paper arms stretch a little while buckling (stubby arms can't otherwise reach across the chest).
- Rendering is slow (~12 min) because of the big car sprites and scrolling scenery.
