# Storyboard blind check, round 1 (sb1)

## Beat by beat
- 0.0 s: Night porch. Bunny draws on a small pumpkin with a blue crayon. Bear is excited (sparkle eyes) and reaches in. Chick watches, happy.
- 1.5 s: Bunny has drawn one curve (an eye). Bear now holds the crayon. Bunny looks surprised ("o" mouth). I read this as Bear grabbing the crayon.
- 3.0 s: Bear draws with his tongue out, too busy to notice. Bunny is close to crying. Chick frowns.
- 4.4 s: Bear sees her face and looks worried ("oh"). Bunny has the crayon again, and Bear's open paw is next to it. I read it as Bear giving it back.
- 6.6 s: Bunny finishes her eye. Everyone smiles.
- 17.3 s: Chick's turn. She draws a heart nose, and Bear's paw passes or steadies the crayon.
- 19.5 s: Bear's turn. He draws the smile, and he and Chick both stick their tongues out.
- 25.5 s: The pumpkin glows as a lantern. Everyone is amazed.

## Problem by 10 s, and its cause
Bear takes the crayon from Bunny, so she is sad. The cause is that he's too excited to wait.

## Feelings
- Bunny: happy, then hurt, then happy and proud.
- Bear: eager, then sorry, then generous.
- Chick: happy, then disapproving, then joining in.

## Lesson
Wait your turn, and if you grab, give it back. Taking turns makes something better together.

## Risky frames (another reading is possible)
- 0.0 to 1.5 s: The grab itself isn't shown. You could also read it as two crayons, with Bear simply drawing beside Bunny. If so, Bunny is sad because he's "drawing on her pumpkin", not because he took her crayon. The snatch needs its own close-up.
- 4.4 s: Bear's paw on the crayon could read as him still tugging at it, not handing it back. The hand-over needs a clear moment.
- 6.6 to 17.3 s: There are no frames for 10 s, so I can't tell how Chick's turn starts or whether that stretch drags.
- 17.3 s: It's unclear whether Bear is giving Chick the crayon or holding it himself.
- 25.5 s: The pumpkin was drawn on, not carved, and nobody puts a light inside it, so the glow reads as magic. It also isn't clear that the glow comes from sharing.

## Funny frames
- 3.0 s: Bear's tongue-out concentration while Bunny is about to cry. The contrast is funny.
- 19.5 s: Bear and Chick both stick their tongues out together, a copycat gag.
- 17.3 s: Little Chick stretching up to draw. Mildly cute.
