# Storyboard blind read, round 3 (sb3)

**Beats**
- 1 s: Sunny afternoon on a porch. Chick, delighted, plays with heart-shaped spots of sunlight that shine through the three heart cut-outs in the gate (one on the wall, one on her tummy).
- 5 s: She touches the heart on her tummy, as if to hold it.
- 8.5 s: Sunset. The hearts fade and her smile goes.
- 10.5 s: Dusk. The hearts are gone and Chick looks worried, still reaching.
- 13.5 s: Night. Chick peeks over the gate, uneasy.
- 88.5-94.5 s: A big human hand finishes a pumpkin with a heart-nose face and drops a lit tea light inside. Bear watches at the left edge.
- 133 s: The lantern throws a huge glowing smiley face with a heart onto the porch wall. Bunny, Chick and Bear look delighted.
- 144 s: Chick hugs the glowing pumpkin, everyone laughs, and the gate hearts glow warm again.

**Problem by 10 s:** Chick loses her heart-shaped lights.
**Cause:** The sun goes down.

**Feelings:** Chick goes from joy (playing with the light) to sad and uneasy (the light is gone, it's dark), then to joy (a bigger heart light, made with friends). Bear and Bunny are curious, then proud and happy.

**Lesson:** When your light goes away, friends can help you make a new one. (This is my guess. The frames don't show who decided to help or why.)

**Frames that could be read another way**
- 1-10.5 s: The heart could look like Chick's own magic or her "love" fading, as if she is ill or heartbroken, instead of sunlight through the gate. The link between the sunbeam and the gate cut-outs needs one clear beat.
- 13.5 s: "Scared of the dark" or "waiting for someone"? Both fit.
- 13.5-88.5 s: A 75 s gap with no frames. Who noticed Chick was sad, whose idea the pumpkin was, and who carved it are all unknown.
- 88.5/94.5 s: The adult hand does the work, so a child may think a grown-up solved it, not the friends.
- 133 s: Bunny holds a white stick (a carving tool or knife?). The pumpkin's face points away from us. Bear stands behind the gate, as if shut out.
