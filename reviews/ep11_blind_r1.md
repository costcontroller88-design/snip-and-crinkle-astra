## Blocking
1. **27.9-28.6 s, harsh audio jump.** The idea sting goes from -22 to -4.5 LUFS momentary in 0.3 s (about 18 dB). Every other cue peaks near -8 LUFS.
2. **0-15 s, the cause of Chick's sadness is unclear.** Bear's eyes are closed throughout, so he reads as sleepy, not as keeping the pumpkin to himself. Chick's wish is shown only by small, pale hearts that blend into the wall (under a tenth of the frame). A child sees "Chick is sad and leaves" but not why, so the lesson has no visible problem to fix. Have Bear actively pull the pumpkin away, and give Chick a close-up as she reaches for it.
3. **13-15 s, Chick's exit.** She disappears behind Bear in about 1 s at mid-frame, so many children will miss that she left. The 16 s reaction depends on it. Hold on her walking away, or show the empty spot.

## Polish
- 26-27 s: Bunny's sad face right after Chick is comforted is confusing. Make it a clear thinking pose.
- 32-80 s: 48 s of calm drawing with no gag. Add one small mishap.
- 80 s: the grown-up hand arrives without setup. Have the friends look up at it.
- 86-95 s: the light is a small white rectangle. Show a clear battery light with a click, so it never reads as a flame.
- 116-124 s: the pumpkin turn is quick. Slow it so the belly projection reads as cause and effect.
- 142-145 s: in the wide shot, the wing is small and orange-on-orange. Lead with the close-up and repeat the peekaboo once.
- 147-150 s: the sparkle touch is tiny. Make it a big high-five.
- After 124 s the pumpkin faces away, so the face the kids drew is never seen again. End on a shot showing both faces.
