# Short plan: "Oops! A Splash of Blue" (vertical, ~43 s)

Written before building. A re-cut of the approved pilot episode (2026-10-06) for the Shorts feed: same
performances, filmed through a vertical camera (1080x1920), tighter framing for phones, and the whole story
kept (cause, sadness, choice, sorry, the fix, the wow) so it is a complete narrative on its own.

## Rules for the cut
- Each beat at least 0.8 s; key beats 1.5-4 s (toddler pace, but the Shorts feed needs momentum).
- Faces sit at about 40% of the frame height; nothing important in the bottom 19% (title/caption) or the
  right 12% (like/share buttons).
- Zoom never above 2x (rendered at 2x: always sharp).
- Sound: the episode's sound effects cut with the pictures (so every action keeps its sound); a new, continuous
  music bed composed for the Short's own timeline (cutting the episode's music would jump). Jingle at the start
  and at the paw-bump. No voices.
- No loop: it ends on the paw-bump and a short hold (a story Short, not an 18 s loop).
