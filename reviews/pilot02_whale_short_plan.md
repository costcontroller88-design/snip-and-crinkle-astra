# Short plan: "Oops! The Splash Becomes a Whale!" (vertical, ~18 s, transformation Short)

Written before building (approved by the owner 2026-10-06: "yes to the whale Short"). It tests the benchmark's
strongest Shorts pattern, one satisfying transformation, against our 43 s story Short. To make it a different video
rather than a shorter copy, the camera stays locked on the painting for most of it, so the viewer watches one picture
change: friends picture → SPLAT → dotted whale → seven painted layers → before/after wipe.

## Rules
- Locked canvas framing: canvas centre at ~44% of the height, 2x zoom (the maximum for a sharp 2x render), canvas
  inside the safe zones (not in the bottom 19% or the right 12%).
- Every beat at least 0.8 s; the hook's action starts on frame 1.
- Sound: the episode's effects cut with the pictures; the wipe gets whoosh + chime + twinkle; a new music bed with the
  jingle at the start and the ending jingle at the paw-bump. No voices. No loop (it ends on the paw-bump).
