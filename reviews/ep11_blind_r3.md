# Blind review, round 3: review_video.mp4 (160 s, 1920x1080)

## What happens (first-time viewer)
- **0-8 s** A chick stands by a garden gate with three heart holes in it. A soft heart glows on her tummy and a big heart glows on the wall beside her (cut off by the left edge). She smiles, sparkles and waves toward the left of the screen. Happy.
- **8-14 s** The sun sets and everything turns purple. Both hearts fade. Her crest droops, she looks worried and walks off to the left. Sad. *Why* the hearts went away is not shown. Nothing on screen links them to the sun or to the gate's heart holes.
- **15-27 s** Cut: Bear and Bunny (with a notepad) are suddenly beside her. They hug her. She is still unsure.
- **28-32 s** Bunny gets an idea (sparkle). She draws a glowing pumpkin with a smiley face and a heart. The chick lights up.
- **33-70 s** Bear brings a pumpkin and draws eyes and a smile on it, tongue out while he concentrates. The chick adds a red heart nose. Everyone is proud.
- **72-84 s** Bunny shows her drawing (now with a little chick beside the pumpkin). A grown-up's hand comes in and gives a thumbs-up.
- **85-100 s** The hand rests on the pumpkin. The drawn lines become carved holes. It lifts off the lid and drops in a battery tea-light, and the pumpkin glows. All three cheer.
- **104-124 s** Bear lifts the pumpkin with its face toward him, and the glowing face shows up on his tummy. He is surprised, then delighted.
- **131-160 s** Wide shot: the pumpkin sits facing the house, and a giant glowing smiley with a heart nose fills the wall. The chick covers part of the light with her wing and the smile half-vanishes (peekaboo), then comes back. At the end she snuggles Bunny and Bear waves from the gate.

**Lesson (as I read it):** When the dark takes your happy light away, friends help you make a new one. This is half-clear. Because the cause of the problem is never shown, it could just as well read "don't be scared of the dark" or "cheer up a sad friend".

**Funny moments:** 31 s Bunny's sparkle idea; 56-62 s Bear's tongue-out concentration; 68 s the chick's tongue-out heart; 84 s the grown-up's thumbs-up; 116-122 s the face appears on Bear's tummy; 141-145 s the chick's wing "erases" the giant smile. All are gentle smiles, and there are only about 2 real laughs (116 s, 142 s) in 160 s.

**What keeps me watching in the first 10 s:** a cute chick with a glowing heart, and then the sky changing colour while the heart fades. It is pretty, but the puzzle "where did the light go?" only works if the child sees where it came from.

## Blocking
1. **0-14 s: the problem has no visible cause.** The tummy heart and wall heart have no light source. There is no sunbeam through the gate's three heart holes (and three small holes against one big heart doesn't match). A child sees "her heart went out" or "she's waving bye to someone" (the wave at 4-11 s points off-screen left). This weakens the ending: the giant heart-nose face on the wall does not land as "we got your heart-light back". Fix: show the sunbeam passing through the gate holes onto her and the wall, have her play with the heart-light, and keep the wall heart fully in frame.
2. **88-90 s: the carving is invisible.** The drawn lines just turn into holes under a resting hand in about 1.5 s. A child can't tell what happened or who did it. Keep the knife off screen, but show the grown-up "doing something" (a cut-away, the hand working behind the pumpkin, the friends watching it happen), then reveal.

No scary or unkind content. The tea-light is battery-powered and the carving is done by a grown-up. Technical: integrated loudness **-14.2 LUFS** (in range), true peak -3.0 dBFS, no silent gaps found at -38 dB / 0.25 s.

## Polish
- 15 s: the friends' arrival is skipped (the chick walks off alone, then hard cut to the group hug). A 1-2 s arrival would help.
- 72-84 s and 100-112 s: about 12 s each of standing and smiling with no new action. Trim, or add a gag.
- 124-131 s: Bear turning the pumpkin toward the wall happens off screen. Show him place it, so "pumpkin face to big face on wall" is caused on screen. The beam cone helps but is soft.
- 141-145 s: the wing-peekaboo is the best gag. Make it bigger and repeat it (the chick makes the smile wink, the friends laugh).
- 156-160 s: Bear waving from outside the gate could read as "Bear is leaving". Fine if intended.
- 84 s: the grown-up's hand arrives with no setup. A brief earlier glimpse (door opening) would help.
- Comedy is thin for 160 s. One more physical gag in the middle section would lift it above 6.
