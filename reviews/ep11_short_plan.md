## b1. Story card
**Lesson: when it's a friend's turn, wait until they finish; then it's your turn.** Wrong → right: Crinkle, bouncing,
eyes on the pumpkin, takes the marker from Snip's paw halfway round the first eye → he sees the
half eye and her empty paw, puts the marker back with its tip where the line stopped, waits (paws behind his back,
copying Pip) and gets his turn from Pip. The marker: Snip → Crinkle (wrong) → Snip → Pip → Crinkle.
**Angle (ROADMAP, quoted):** "A plain pumpkin gets a paper/paint face and glows (battery light placed by the
grown-up's paper hand)"; Short: "a 20-30 s 'Plain Pumpkin Becomes a Glowing Friend!' transformation Short".
**Engine:** B (Crinkle errs; trait 3). **Setting:** Ep 11 porch, early evening. **Outcome:** a face drawn in three
turns glows as one face. **Wow:** the whole paper lights; black lines stay dark. **Music:** glock lead (music box), pluck bass, organ pad under the glow; F major, 104 bpm,
straight. **Safety:** uncapped markers, tips only on the lantern; the take is a slow pinch, never a tug; only the grown-up touches the tea
light (white body, side switch, LED dome, never opened, steady glow, no flame, flicker or smoke), in through the top ring, stays inside; no candle, cutting, tool or climbing; the
glow is a warm wash, no beam on a face; only Crinkle laughs at Crinkle. From 21.2 nobody touches the lantern: no paw, wing or foot on the paper or over the top ring; Crinkle's
wiggle-dance arms stay on the side away from it; Pip dances in place (gate 1, F3).

2. Her empty paw; ears droop. 3. He sees the half eye, then her teary face. 4. He gives it back, tip on the line's end; bow; her nod (6.4: the fix).
