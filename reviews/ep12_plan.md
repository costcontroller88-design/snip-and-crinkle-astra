## 1. Story card
**Lesson: every toy has a home; when you finish with a toy, put it back.** Wrong (r2): Snip drops each toy where it is (a truck leaves his paw as he rushes); carrying a cushion to sit on, he looks back at his drum and lets it drop at his feet, onto Pip's ball, unseen. Right: the three put every toy in its colour basket, 1-2-3; under the last thing, the cushion, is the ball; Pip puts it home.
**Angle (ROADMAP topic 2, quoted):** "Engine A (Snip errs: his mess buries Pip's ball by accident; the three put toys in picture baskets, 1-2-3, and the ball appears). One new thing: the playroom."
**Engine:** A. **Setting:** playroom toy corner, morning. **Outcome:** floor clear, ball found. **Wow:** pull-back on the clear floor, the ball rolls straight across. **Music:** steel pan, marimba bass, G major, 112 bpm. **Safety (r2):** nobody climbs, enters, sits in or leans over a basket; nothing on a neck or scarf; sit-downs on bare floor; nobody laughs at Snip.
